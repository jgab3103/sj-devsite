Parama Sapta-Na Da Jayanthi

The words “Parama Guru” are no longer in current use. However, they have been retained here because they formed part of the original version of this chant as it was gifted to Adi Da Samraj during His Lifetime, as a recorded devotional offering from the New Zealand Adidam community. The words may now be replaced with “Parama-Sapta-Na”.

Da Jayanthi

Da Jayanthi celebrates the human birth of Adi Da, who was born on November 3, 1939, in Jamaica, New York. The word “Jayanthi” comes from Sanskrit and means “Victorious.”
