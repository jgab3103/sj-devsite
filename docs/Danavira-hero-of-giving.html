Danavira, Hero Of Giving

This chant has been composed for use during the Danavira Mela period.

Danavira Mela, or "The Season of Light-In-Everybody," is a major festive season in Adidam running from early November to January 11th. It focuses on recognizing divine light in all people, gift-giving, and celebrating the "Divine Self-Emergence" of Adi Da Samraj.
