Om Sri Parama-Sapta-Na

This chant is composed using 4 of the 23 formal mantras of invocation in Adidam.

Any one of these mantras are sufficient to be used alone and can be used repetitively.

  • Om Sri Parama-Sapta-Na Adi Da Love-Ananda Hridayam
  • Om Da Om
  • Om Ma Da
  • Om Sri Da
