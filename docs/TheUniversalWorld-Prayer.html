The Universal World-Prayer

The Universal World-Prayer Chant has been composed as a Call and Response chant. Note that because there are ‘pick up’ notes for some of the lines, and there is a lot of text involved it is recommended that it remains a contemplative chant to give full meaning and space for the words to be heard, felt and received. Many of these slow contemplative chants are best suited to be played with a ‘half-time feel’.
