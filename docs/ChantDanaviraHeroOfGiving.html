Danavira Mela
Danavira Mela, or “The Season of Light-In-Everybody,” is a major festive season in Adidam observed from early November through January 11. This sacred season emphasises recognising the divine light present in all people, the spirit of gift-giving, and the celebration of the “Divine Self-Emergence” of Adi Da Samraj.
