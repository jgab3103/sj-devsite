Love-Ananda Adi Da Bhagavan

This chant combines elements of both Simple Name Invocation and Ruchira Mahamantras. It is not usual to mix the two. Therefore, if desired, the phrases in lines 3 and 4 — “Da Om” and “Da Om Om Da” — may be replaced with “Adi Da” or “Adi Guru” in order to maintain a more consistent Simple Name Invocation form throughout.

Adidam Ruchira Dham

Adidam Ruchira Dham is the full name of the “Radical” Reality-Way of Adidam. Here, it refers to the annual month of celebrations held each April, commemorating both the beginning of Adi Da’s public teaching work in 1972 and His “Return” to “Brightness.” This culminating Yogic Event in Avatar Adi Da’s Teaching Work took place on April 12, 2000, on Lopez Island, Washington. Since that Event, His participation in this earthly realm has been understood as a Spiritual Radiance into His body and into the world — from Infinitely Above. With the Lopez Island Event, Avatar Adi Da Samraj utterly “returned” to the same “Bright” State He knew in infancy. He now exists at the threshold between the Divine Domain and the earthly realm. From that “Position,” He continues, as always, to participate with passionate urgency and utmost love in the “heart-breaking companionship of mortal beings,” ceaselessly Spiritually Working to Bless and Liberate all beings.
