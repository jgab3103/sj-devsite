Parama Guru Da Jayanthi

The words ‘Parama Guru’ are no longer used. However this was the original chant and words when gifted to Adi Da Samraj, during His Lifetime. as a recorded offering from the New Zealand Adidam community. The words can be replaced with: ‘Parama-Sapta-Na’.
